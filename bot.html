4 · How it works (the honest version)

LINE is connected officially (Messaging API: the bot is invited into the group as a member) — this covers both Thai groups at once, with no risk. The Russian guides' WhatsApp is the next step, via a dedicated read-only bridge number. The classifier is dictionary-based, built on the team's real vocabulary, with optional AI enrichment of unrecognized messages; severity (blood / evacuation / repeated risk to a customer) is escalated automatically. All data stays on the company's server; the bot never posts in the groups. The same events feed the live operations dashboard →
