What this demo shows

  • Guide app

    Choose the trip and team colour, then send the photos — originals, straight from the phone.

  • Automatic check

    Each photo is checked on arrival: date, trip, team and file. No manual sorting.

  • Tourist gallery

    One QR on every wristband. The tourist picks the day, trip and team colour and sees the group's photos.

  • Find my jet ski

    Type the number from the jet ski — photos where it is visible move to the top.

About this demo

The photos here are generated placeholders with the jet ski number painted on. You can also send your own photos from this phone. Everything stays in this browser — nothing is uploaded.
